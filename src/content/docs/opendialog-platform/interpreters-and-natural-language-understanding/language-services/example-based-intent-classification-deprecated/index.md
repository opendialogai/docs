---
title: Example-based intent classification [Deprecated]
---

The OpenDialog Intent classifier is an LLM-powered intent classification service. It enables you to provide examples of phrases and entities and then classify intents based on that.

:::caution
We recommend the use of the [semantic classifier](/opendialog-platform/interpreters-and-natural-language-understanding/language-services/semantic-intent-classifier) going forward rather than the example phrase -based intent classifier.
:::
